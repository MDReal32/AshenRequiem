# 📖 Modpack Development Plan – Boundless Horizons

## 🌌 Lore Arc

* **Step 1 – Deadlands:** You awaken in a world of ash and bone, cursed to survive among ruins of the fallen.
* **Step 2 – Overworld:** You escape into a land of life and growth, yet its beauty hides forgotten scars.
* **Step 3 – Twilight/Fey Realm:** Strange lights guide you through crystalline forests, where secrets of magic linger.
* **Step 4 – Advanced Deadlands:** The veil lifts — the true Deadlands reveals its hidden heart, demanding tribute.
* **Step 5 – Sky Realm:** Beyond the clouds lies perilous freedom, where only mastery of power can keep you alive.
* **Step 6 – Ascended Plane:** At last, you ascend to the plane of creation itself, where survival gives way to boundless shaping.

---

## Core Vision

* [ ] Player starts in **Deadlands** (harsh survival).
* [ ] Progression = **multi-dimensional bases**.
* [ ] Final dimension unlocks **Creative-like toolkit** (earned sandbox freedom).

---

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Worlds

### Step 1 – Deadlands (Spawn World)

* [ ] Worldgen: barren, ash, bones.
* [ ] Starter resources (bonewood, ashstone, soul essence).
* [ ] Grind → craft **Portal of Escape**.
* [ ] Structures: ruins, bone towers, corrupted shrines.
* [ ] Mobs: corrupted husks, bone crawlers.
* [ ] Boss: Keeper of Ashes (drops portal key).
* [ ] Progress unlock → **Overworld portal**.

---

### Step 2 – Overworld (Contrast World)

* [ ] Normal world, fertile, green.
* [ ] Farming + villagers.
* [ ] Recipes require Deadlands essences.
* [ ] Structures: abandoned villages, ruined temples.
* [ ] Goal → build **Twilight/Fey Portal**.

---

### Step 3 – Twilight/Fey Realm

* [ ] Biomes: glowing forests, crystal lakes.
* [ ] Resources: mystic catalysts, crystal shards.
* [ ] Structures: fairy groves, crystal obelisks.
* [ ] Boss: Fey Guardian (drops portal heart).
* [ ] Requires Deadlands + Overworld materials to survive here.
* [ ] Unlock **Advanced Deadlands return**.

---

### Step 4 – 🔒 Advanced Deadlands (Hidden Layer)

* [ ] Exists **within Deadlands**, but masked until unlock.
* [ ] Appears ordinary pre-unlock (biomes/blocks/structures disguised).
* [ ] Players can wander here freely but see only **decoys**.
* [ ] **Unlock item/event** flips reveal → true advanced biomes, loot, mobs appear.
* [ ] Veil Blocks scatter everywhere; breaking pre-unlock causes **permanent scars**.
* [ ] Advanced Deadlands yields **rare essences/keys** needed for Sky Realm portal.
* [ ] Optional mini-boss or challenge encounter upon reveal.

---

### Step 5 – Sky Realm (Aether-like)

* [ ] Floating islands, dangerous void.
* [ ] Focus: automation, power systems.
* [ ] Resources: cloudsteel, aether crystals.
* [ ] Structures: sky fortresses, floating dungeons.
* [ ] Boss: Sky Warden (gives Ascension Core).
* [ ] Unlock **Final Realm portal**.

---

### Step 6 – Ascended Plane (Final Dimension)

* [ ] A serene infinite void, glowing fields.
* [ ] Freedom hub = player’s final base.
* [ ] Unlock **Creative-like toolkit**:

    * [ ] Builder’s Gauntlet (instant build/break).
    * [ ] Infinity Pouch (infinite blocks once unlocked).
    * [ ] Wings of Freedom (unlimited flight).
    * [ ] Worldshaper’s Wand (area edits).
    * [ ] Time Anchor (control weather/day).
* [ ] All tools require **materials from every world**.
